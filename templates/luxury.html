By Royal Proclamation

A Union of Hearts

Be it known to all persons of gentle heart and noble spirit, that on the twelfth day of June in the year two thousand and twenty-seven, the houses of Genevieve and Alexander shall be joined in holy matrimony.

The ceremony shall unfold within the elegant courtyards of Courtyard Shillong, amidst the pine-clad hills of Meghalaya, where candlelight, roses and the music of strings shall bear witness to their vows.

We beseech you to grace us with your company, that the evening may be complete and our joy made whole.

Genevieve & Alexander

The Evening

A Royal Timeline

Touch each hour to reveal its secret.

Guests are received on the terrace with vintage champagne, canapés and the soft murmur of a string quartet.

Beneath the elegant canopy of the Courtyard Lawn, Genevieve and Alexander exchange their vows before family and friends.

As the sun lowers over the pine-clad hills, join us in the gardens for photographs, oysters and a toast to the newlyweds.

A five-course dinner served in the Grand Ballroom, beneath the hotel's elegant vaulted ceiling.

The orchestra strikes up the first waltz, and the floor opens to all who wish to dance until dawn.

A final flourish over the hills — gold and crimson light across the sky, and a toast to the years ahead.

Dress Code

White Tie & Tiaras

Gentlemen are requested to wear black tailcoats with white bow ties.

Ladies are invited to wear floor-length gowns, long gloves and heirloom jewels.

We ask only that you avoid white, which is reserved for the bride.
