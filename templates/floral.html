Our Story

Where it all began

It began with a wrong turn down a rain-washed lane in Shillong and a shared umbrella that neither of them needed. Amara was chasing a bakery she had read about; Elias was chasing a tea stall a friend had sworn was the best in the hills.

They found neither. They found each other instead, and talked until the rain stopped and the shutters came down and the streetlights hummed on.

— and we never really stopped talking.

Six summers later, on a terrace above the mist, Elias asked a question he had been carrying in his pocket for eleven weeks. Amara said yes before he had finished the sentence.

Now we are gathering the people we love most in a garden of roses and old stone, to begin the next chapter — and we would be lost without you there.

With all our love, A & E
