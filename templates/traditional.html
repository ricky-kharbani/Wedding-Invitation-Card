Dress Code

What to Wear

Dress in the colours of celebration. Comfort is welcome; tradition is cherished.

Mehndi & Sangeet

Festive Indian — think marigold, emerald and fuchsia. Comfortable shoes for dancing.

Haldi

Shades of yellow and white. Expect turmeric — choose something you can laugh about later.

Vivah

Traditional Indian formal — maroon, gold and ivory. Kindly avoid black and white.

Reception

Formal Indian or Western. Deep tones and a little sparkle are always welcome.
