01 — The Story

Two lives, one long conversation.

It began in a bookshop in Shillong, somewhere between the poetry shelf and the last copy of a novel neither of them had heard of. Genevieve was reaching for it. Alexander already had it. They agreed to share.

Six years, four cities and one very patient dog later, they are choosing the hills they met in to say the words out loud — surrounded by the people who made them who they are.
